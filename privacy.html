ZarPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

ZarPulse is a YouTube news channel. The ZarPulse app uses YouTube API Services only to manage the ZarPulse channel itself: uploading the channel's own videos and Shorts, updating the title of the channel's live stream, and reading public messages in the channel's own live chat so they can be shown on air.

  • The app is used only by the ZarPulse channel owner. It does not ask viewers to sign in.
  • Public live chat messages (display name and message text) are read from the YouTube API to show viewer comments and Prediction League votes on screen. They are not sold or shared with anyone.
  • Access tokens are stored privately on the ZarPulse server and are never published.
  • No personal data is sold, and no advertising profiles are built.

By watching or commenting on ZarPulse you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at Google security settings.
